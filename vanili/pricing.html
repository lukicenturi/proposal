How we price

Prices you can plan around

Formulas and clauses, written into the contract. Buyers who lived through the 2017 to 2019 spike want to know how a price will behave before they sign, so these are the rules every bulk contract follows.

  • A formula, not a one-off figure

    Bulk prices follow a published discount to the equivalent Madagascar grade and are reviewed every quarter against a transparent index, so you can see why a price moved.

  • A price-escalation clause after six months

    Every contract longer than six months includes a price-escalation clause. The vanilla cycle will turn, and both sides should know in advance how a turn is handled.

  • Duty-free today, with a pass-through clause

    Whole vanilla beans (HS 0905.10) are duty-free at MFN rates in the US, Canada and Australia today. Our contracts carry a tariff pass-through clause, so a duty introduced after signing is handled by a written rule instead of a renegotiation.

  • 30 percent deposit on new bulk accounts

    New bulk accounts pay a deposit with the order. Established accounts move to agreed payment terms.

  • Credit insurance on larger accounts

    Larger open-account balances are covered by credit insurance, which keeps supply steady for every buyer when one pays late.
