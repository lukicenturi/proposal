Request a quote or a graded sample. Grade, quantity and destination are enough for a first price, and we ask for anything else we need. We reply within one business day.

Who you are

Prefer email? hello@luxvanilla.com

Your request is on our desk

We reply within one business day, Monday to Friday, Jakarta time (WIB). If we need a detail to price the lot, we will ask by email.

Your email app opened with the request filled in

Press send there and the request reaches us. If no email app opened, write to hello@luxvanilla.com with the product, quantity and destination.

What happens next

  1. 1

    We confirm within one business day and ask for anything missing.

  2. 2

    A quote for your grade, quantity and destination, FOB Jakarta or CIF to your port, naming the lot it ships from. Samples go out with their CoA.

  3. 3

    A 25 kg trial lot, then a twelve-month contract once it passes your inspection.

How we price

Indonesian vanilla and cocoa, hand-picked at full maturity, slowly cured, tested lot by lot and exported worldwide.
