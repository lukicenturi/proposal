Why we started

A second origin, done properly

Between 2017 and 2019, vanilla buyers who relied on a single Madagascar harvest watched the price of their key ingredient multiply, with no second origin ready to take up the slack.

Since then, nearly every large buyer has written origin diversification into its sourcing policy. Prices have fallen back, but the lesson stayed: a second origin is only useful if it is documented, consistent and available when the first one fails.

We started LuxVanilla to be that origin. Indonesian vanilla has always had the flavour; what buyers missed was a grower and exporter who documents every lot. That is our promise.

Why Indonesia

Harvested and cured all year

Indonesia is the only major origin that harvests and cures all year round.

  • Already trusted

    Indonesia is already the number-three origin for US vanilla buyers. Extract houses know the beans; what they have lacked is a grower and exporter that documents every lot.

  • All-year supply

    We harvest and cure through the year, so supply does not hang on one crop or one season.

  • A distinct flavour

    Indonesian planifolia, cured well, has a smoky, woody profile that pastry chefs can tell apart from Bourbon. It works particularly well in chocolate, coffee and spirits.

Why LuxVanilla

Our promise, lot by lot

The quality system is the product. Indonesian vanilla lost buyers for known reasons: uneven curing, high moisture, occasional mould and beans picked before they were mature. Each promise here answers one of them.

  • A published grading standard

    We grade every bean by hand, by length and appearance, to a written standard you can read before you buy.

  • An accredited-lab certificate of analysis per lot

    Vanillin by HPLC, moisture, water activity, mould and yeast, aflatoxin, heavy metals and a pesticide multi-residue screen.

  • Re-graded and vacuum-packed at origin

    Food-grade pouches in lined cartons, each labelled with origin, harvest window and lot number.

  • Failed lots never ship as Grade A

    A lot that fails is sold as extraction grade or rejected to the supplier under our agreement.

  • A full-time QC manager in Indonesia

    On the ground at the curing house, not reviewing paperwork from abroad.

How we work

From our plantation to your port

One company from the vine to the export documents. The people who grade the beans are the same people who answer for them when they arrive.

  1. Indonesia

    Our plantation and partner farmers

    We grow vanilla and cocoa with a community of farmers we know by name. Vanilla is hand-picked only at full maturity.

  2. Indonesia

    Curing, grading and testing

    Beans are cured slowly, graded by hand and tested by an accredited laboratory, then vacuum-packed and labelled by lot.

  3. Worldwide

    Export by sea or air

    FOB Jakarta, CIF or CFR to your port, or air freight for small lots, with the CoA, phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin and halal certificate on every shipment.

Where we are going

Growing, carefully

  • More partner farmers

    We bring new farmer groups into the same grading and testing system, so supply can grow without the standard slipping.

  • A second curing-house partner

    Two curing houses instead of one, so a single partner's bad season never interrupts supply.
