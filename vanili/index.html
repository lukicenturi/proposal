About us

Rooted in Indonesian soil

LuxVanilla is an Indonesian producer and exporter of vanilla and cocoa, from our farmers to your production line.

We work from our own plantation and with a community of partner farmers who know these vines and trees by heart. Our vanilla is hand-picked only at full maturity, then cured slowly until the beans turn dark, supple and fragrant.

Before anything ships, we grade it by hand to a published standard and send every lot to an accredited laboratory. The certificate of analysis travels with the beans, wherever in the world they go.

Quality

Tested, traceable, trusted

Three promises we keep on every lot we ship.

Tested lot by lot

We sample every lot and send it to an accredited laboratory: vanillin by HPLC, moisture, water activity, mould and yeast, aflatoxin, heavy metals and a pesticide multi-residue screen. The certificate of analysis travels with the lot.

Traceable to the farmer

We print origin, harvest window and lot number on every pack, and each lot traces back to the farmer group that grew it. A lot that fails is never shipped as Grade A.

Exported worldwide

We ship from Indonesia by sea or air: FOB Jakarta, CIF or CFR to your port, or air freight for small lots. Every shipment carries its CoA, phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin and halal certificate.
