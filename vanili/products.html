Family 01 · four grades

Our vanilla

Vanilla planifolia

We hand-pick our planifolia at full maturity and cure it slowly. The result is a smoky, woody profile that pastry chefs can tell apart from Bourbon, and that shines in chocolate, coffee and spirits.

Pictured: green vanilla pods on the vine, months before curing.

We harvest and cure all year, so every grade is available every month. Each grade below is defined by our published grading standard and confirmed on the lot's certificate of analysis.

Packing, labels and storage

Packed with care

We re-grade and pack at origin, so beans reach you as supple as the day they were cured.

  • Bulk and trade

    Vacuum-packed in 1 kg and 5 kg food-grade pouches inside lined cartons, re-graded and packed at origin.

  • Retail packs

    2, 5 or 10 beans in glass tubes or pouches, and 250 g and 1 kg bags.

  • On every pack

    Origin, harvest window and lot number, so each pack traces back to its certificate of analysis.

  • Storage

    15 to 20 °C and controlled humidity, at every stage from the curing house to export.
