WHAT WE DO

THE BRO
TREATMENT.

One stop for paint protection and perfection. Tell us what your car needs, or send a photo on WhatsApp and we'll tell you straight, no upsell drama.

  1. 01

    PAINT PROTECTION FILM

    Self-healing film that eats rock chips and scratches so your paint doesn't have to. Gloss or matte, high-impact zones or full body, applied by our PPF specialists at @bro.ppf.

  2. 02

    NANO CERAMIC COATING

    Our specialty. A hard nano-ceramic layer bonded to your paint: deep wet gloss, UV and water-spot resistance, and way easier washes for years, not weeks.

  3. 03

    FINEST PAINT REFINEMENT

    Multi-stage machine polishing that erases swirls, holograms and dull haze before anything gets coated. This is where the mirror finish is actually made.

  4. 04

    INTERIOR & ENGINE BAY

    Deep-clean detailing for cabin and engine bay: seats, trim, carpets and every vent, so the inside feels as fresh as the outside looks.

  5. 05

    VELG & GLASS DETAILING

    Wheels detailed to the barrel and nano glass coating for clear vision in the rain. The details most shops skip are the ones we obsess over.

  6. 06

    FREE PICK UP & DELIVERY

    Too busy? We collect your car, work our magic, and bring it back gleaming. Zero effort on your side, that's the whole point.
