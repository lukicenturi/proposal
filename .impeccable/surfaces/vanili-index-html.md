# Surface brief: vanili/ (LuxVanilla multipage site)

Scope: a real multipage site for LuxVanilla, an Indonesian vanilla and cocoa supplier selling direct to importers, extract houses, flavour companies and artisan makers in the US, Canada and Australia. Pages: index, products, process, pricing, about, contact, quote. English only this round. Visitor mode: Persuade (home, products, pricing, about), Operate (contact and quote forms).

Audience: a vanilla or cocoa sourcing manager at an extract house, importer-distributor or flavour company, plus artisan gelato, pastry, distillery and roaster buyers. They buy on specification and want a documented second origin with local stock. Goal action: request a quote or sample.

Proof on hand: the business plan (vanili/Vanilla_Business_Plan.pdf): published grading spec, accredited-lab CoA per lot, vacuum packing, Singapore hub, stock in Los Angeles and Toronto, year-round harvest. No prices, no street addresses, no testimonials. Cocoa specs indicative. Contact details placeholders.

Constraints: no demo badge, no gallery card, noindex, mobile-first, no em dashes, Tailwind CDN plus Google Fonts, assets inside vanili/assets only, Web3Forms with mailto fallback.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, played straight: the premium Indonesian exporter site the buyer expects (derfish.com is the structural model), executed at full craft. The user rejected the Certificate of Analysis concept (seed 5dd66b19, pick card) on 2026-10-27 as AI-ish; the standing exit (canon) is now the commitment.

OWN-WORLD: Black and gold from the logo: near-black grounds for hero, product list and quality bands, ivory for about, process and contact; gold accent for key words, rules, primary buttons. EB Garamond headings, Source Sans 3 body, letterspaced sans small caps for labels, no monospace. Full-bleed photography with dark gradient overlays; product entries as photo-led rows with spec lines.

STORY: The buyer lands on a dark vanilla hero, reads the offer in one line, scrolls derfish-style through about, products with specs, the six-stage process, quality proof, and reaches a contact grid with a form; requests a quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed dark photo of cured vanilla beans, logo top-left in the nav, label line, serif headline with one gold word, one-paragraph sub, three buttons (Explore products, Request a quote, Contact us), stats strip along the foot.

FORM: Category standard (canon) chosen by the user over the roll and the pick; seed key 5dd66b19. Signature interaction: derfish-style staggered reveal on scroll, nothing else.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
